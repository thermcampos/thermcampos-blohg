hybrid work
-----------

I decided (not entirely by choice) to go regularly to a coworking space near my
apartment. By regularly, I mean at least three times a week. The reasons are
noisy constructions at my building, constructions inside our apartment, and
giving my wife some space. (our apartment is very small, and she's in a
graduate program, needs silence to study).

I gotta admit, I thought I'd hate, but actually I can see its value now.
Getting out of the house is doing me so much good. I like the walking commute.
The space is on the fourth floor with a nice view, and there's free coffee
included! 

This is unexpected from me because I've been working from home since 2019, 7
years now, and until recently I was strongly refusing to go back to any office.
But here I am, and I'm pretty happy. Anyways, let's see what the recommendations
section brought this time.
